Report of the trustees for the year ended 31 August 2018
The Trustees submit their annual report and the financial statements for the year ended 31st
August 2018.
The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies set out in
note 1 to the accounts and comply with the charity's trust deed, the Charities Act 2011 and Accounting
and Reporting by Charities: Statement of Recommended Practice applicable to charities preparing
their acounts in accordance with the Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and Republic of
Ireland published on 16 July 2014.
Objectives and activities for the public benefit and grant making policy.
The Trust was originally formed under Deed on 20
th
October 1978 and is a charity organised under the
laws of England and Wales. Its object is to advance public education by means of providing scholarships
and bursaries for the pupils of Aiglon College and other similar educational establishments as well providing
other financial support to the College. Scholarships and bursaries are awarded subject to funds being available
available on the recommendation of the relevant Headmaster. Other grants are made at the discretion of the
Trustees for specific projects generally capital in nature.
Trustees
During the year ended 31st
August 2018 the Trustees were as detailed on the index page.
Trustees, who are appointed under Deed, are directly and solely responsible for the promotion of the
charity’s objects. They meet periodically to discharge their duties.
Principal activity and review of the year
The Statement of Financial Activities is set out on page 6. The results including realised gains for the period
showed a deficit of £16,861 (2017 surplus £14,616) after charitable disbursements of £136,745 (2017: £28,528).
Financial Review
The Charity has received generous donations during the year, allowing it to make transfers of £127,703 (note 2, page 10)
to Aiglon School during the year.
Investment Policy and performance
Surplus funds are invested or placed on bank deposit in order to achieve the best possible returns consistent
with security, while maintaining sufficient liquidity. Having regard to the current financial climate
the Trustees consider the investment performance to be satisfactory.
Risk Management
The major risks to which Friends of Aiglon College UK is exposed as identified by the trustees, have been
reviewed and a system has been established to mitigate those risks.
Reserves policy
The trustees aim to retain sufficient reserves for the foreseeable management and operational expenses of the
charity, while using remaining funds for educational purposes by way of grants and other payments as the
trustees see best for the charity.

Plans for the future
The charity intends to establish and develop stronger relationships with Aiglon alumni that are residing or visiting the UK
by hosting events. These are planning to run in conjunction with Aiglon College’s alumni UK events so resources are shared
where possible, relationships are developed and cross-fundraising opportunities maximised; whilst highlighting the
uniqueness of the FOAC UK and gift aid potential regarding income. A digital platform for FOAC UK is to be sought
and set up so to aide this, along with an FOAC UK alumni database with social media and electronic communication
(e-Newsletter) to reach out and steward past, existing and future donors.
Structure, governance and management
Friends of Aiglon College UK is a registered charity, number 276796.
The Designated Fund represents the 2001 Endowment Fund (“Giving 2001”) and has been separately
designated by the Trustees in order that gifts can be identified and disbursed in accordance with the intentions
of the donors.
The Devonport Fund represents the endowment from the Devonport family which created the Trust in 1978.
The net income arising, after charging the expenses of running the Trust, is available to provide scholarships
and bursaries for the pupils of Aiglon College and other similar educational establishments in furtherance of its
primary object of advancing public education. The following assumption has been made regarding this fund:
All opening assets at 1
st
September 2001 were part of the Devonport Fund because everything received in the
past from other sources was paid out or, at most, the amounts retained were of no significance.
Key management personnel remuneration
The Trust’s regulations do not permit the payment of remuneration to Trustees. The Trustees are entitled to
receive reimbursement of reasonable expenses within approved budgets for expenses related to travel and
subsistence. In 2017 these amounted to Nil (2016 - nil).

NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
For the year ending 31 August 2018
1. Accounting policies
(a) Basis of preparation and assessment of going concern
The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with Accounting and Reporting by Charities:
Statement of Recommended Practice applicable to charities preparing their accounts in accordance with
the Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and Republic of Ireland
(effective 1 January 2015) - Charities SORP (FRS 102), the Financial Reporting Standard applicable
in the UK and Republic of Ireland (FRS 102) and the Charities Act 2011.
The trust constitutes a public benefit entity as defined by FRS102.
The trustees consider that there are no material uncertainties about the Trust's ability to continue as
a going concern.
(b) Reconciliation with previous Generally Accepted Accouning Practice
In preparing the accounts, the trustees have considered whether in applying the accounting policies
required by FRS102 and the Charities SORP FRS 102 a restatement of comparative items was needed.
No restatements were required.
(c) Funds structure
The original beneficent gift of the Devonport Family upon which this Trust is built, is treated as a Restricted
capital fund. All other gifts are treated as Unrestricted and as such are credited to Revenue, as opposed to
Capital, and are available for distribution albeit in accordance with the donors’ wishes.
The Statement of Financial Activities includes donations received under covenant including income tax
recoverable, other non-recurring and small donations, income from investments and interest on bank deposits.
Gifts receivable are credited to income in the period to which they relate.
Any surplus arising from unrestricted funds is treated as a grant to Aiglon College and carried forward as an
intention until remitted.
(d) Income recognition
All income including interest is recognised once the charity has entitlement to that income, it is
probable that the amount will be received and the income can be measured reliably.
(e)Expenditure recognition: is made on an accruals basis once there is a legal or other obligation
committing the charity to that expenditure.
No provision is made for taxation as, in the opinion of the Trustees, no liability to UK taxation will arise.
