Trustees Annual Report Jan 1* 2013 — Dec 31* 2013
2013 was a year where we continued to hold onto all that God has promised without necessarily seeing huge
breakthroughs in specific areas. We have again seen steady growth with people moving into the area and
people becoming Christians through the ministry of the church.
Summary of 2013.
Alpha - In 2013 We looked to run 2 Alpha courses. Our spring course was a real encouragement with lively
discussion and teaching for all involved. We saw commitments and two baptisms, which were a real blessing.
The autumn course was launched at All Bar One in Sutton. Several people signed up for the course but for
various reasons were not able to attend.
Men’s ministry — This continues to grow and good friendship and support is being developed to both
married and single men. This is achieved through socialising together over breakfasts, playing badminton,
visits to the cinema etc. We also have a men'’s life group that meets every other week in the day for support
and friendship. We are looking to develop this further in 2014 with someone taking responsibility for
developing this ministry.
Women’s ministry — This continues to grow as the married and single women come together with social
events, specifically targeted meetings to help support, establish friendships with the demands of modern
life. The women’s ministry has continued to flourish. There have been ongoing events such as the single
ladies Sunday lunches and the book club. We also enjoyed joining in with other New Ground churches’
women’s events — such as a Saturday with Wendy Mann at Everyday Church, Wimbledon and another with
Livvy Gibbs and Wendy Virgo at East Grinstead. A small group of us also travelled into London to the HTB
Free to Be women’s conference. Back in Sutton, we had a fun craft and cake coffee morning and this
November saw the first ladies breakfast of this season.
Social Action — We are part of Community works in Sutton: http://suttoncommunityworks.org/ where, as a
church we are committed both practically and financially. As and eldership team we contribute to the
ongoing development of the social action that they coordinate in Sutton. We also have members of our
congregation who are involved in both foodbank and street pastors and are regularly volunteering to help
with the collection and distribution of food and the late nights out on the streets at the weekend. We have
also had a few opportunities to give help in practical areas like gardening, decorating etc through being on
contacts in this area of ministry. We also have one lady now helping part-time at Age UK and continue to
work with them where we can.
Children’s Work — This continues to grow and develop. We have recently appointed new children’s work
team leaders who are developing the curriculum, worship and activites for the children to enjoy. The
emphasis is to teach the children to be God smart (know the bible and what it teaches) as well as God
connected (pray, connect and develop their relationship with God) as well, of course, having fun along the
way.
Youth Work. — Our youth leaders are developing a good relationship with the youth and having a significant
input into their lives and helping them to cope with all the pressures our youth face in today’s culture. They
have taken the youth to weekend events to help support them and help them develop their faith as well as
offer a youth preach once a month to give them greater opportunity to access the word of God and consider
how to apply it.
Leadership — The leadership team has been strengthened with John Hall being appointed as an Elder. Also
the Senior Pastor has also been seeking to develop future leaders by setting up a core team of leaders from
within the church with the aim of appointing another full time Elder in 2014.
Finances — Finances— Regular gift aided giving rose by 5% during the year with 79% of member households
making monthly payments by standing order. Income to the vision fund fell, maybe as a result of long
protracted negotiations with the vendors for the purchase of Highfield Hall. We continue to apply biblical
principles in our own giving by passing on 10%-12% of our income to support local good causes including
Sutton Foodbank, Street Pastors, Community Works, Sutton Schools work as well as mission activities
oversees.
Total income for the year was £140,265 including £14,695 to the vision fund. Total expenditure reduced by
15.6% to £108,111 as a result of the former Senior Pastor working part time as reported last year. The
surplus on activities in 2013 was £34,274 leaving current assets of £162,338, a healthy sum which is targeted
for the purchase of Highfield Hall in 2014.
Building — Our vision is still to have our own building and we continue to proceed with the acquisition of
Highfield Hall which has been a long and drawn out process. We believe the building will help us serve the
community and the church in lots of different ways. It has been a long time coming but God has been
teaching us many things through the process. We trust that in 2014 we will secure the building. We also
have a vision fund, for which people pay regularly into, which will financially help secure the building when
we finally move in.
Overseas — We continue to support the work of Newfrontiers and help with crisis needs as and when they
arrive. This year we have also had couples, families and singles move overseas also to continue the work that
they feel called to and we continue to lift them up in our prayers. We have held an international day in the
summer, where the focus is overseas mission and having fun and food from all over the world together. We
also give a significant amount of time praying from the nations around the world.
Building community — We are quite a diverse church of many nations and we continue to celebrate that
diversity yet oneness. We do this through various social and community events establishing community and
friendship. We have a number of life groups within church that look to build relationally with each other,
with their neighbours and colleagues and befriend visitors we receive when we meet together on a Sunday.
We look forward to 2014 in the anticipation of acquiring a building which will help us further fulfil our calling
in Sutton and beyond to be a church in the community for the community as well as being a home and base
for church activities.
