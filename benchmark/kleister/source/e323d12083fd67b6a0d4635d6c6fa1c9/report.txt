Annual Report for Charity Commission 2014-2015
The Committee consists of the following individuals
Mr T Aldndge (Chairman)
Mrs C Aldridge (Vice-Chairman)
Mr D Wyett (Treasurer) '
Mrs M Hall (Secretary)
Mrs E Drain
The committee oversee the running of the Community Centre but does not organise any of the functions
that take place. We supply the building and facilities for various groups to use.
The centre continues to be used by a wide variety of groups and has never been busier. There is a Pre-
School that uses the centre each weekday apart from Monday afternoon. Other regular users are Whist
Drive, Karate, Street Dance School, Dog Training, Puppy Training, Church Services, Slimming World,
Tang Sou (martial arts). The film club that held meetings every other Thursday evening have had to stop
due to lack of finances.
The centre has become a popular venue for children’s parties at weekends. Some regular meetings and
informal gatherings also take place.
The centre is also used as a Polling Station when required.

Report on a Meeting of the Holding Trustee with Officers of the Davis Estate

In my last few reports as holding trustee, I have referred to a standard document which I devised some
ten years ago in December 2005. This document has acted as a useful checklist for the annual review
meeting between the Holding Trustees and the Officers of the Davis Estate Community Centre.
As usual we discussed numerous issues under the following broad headings:-
e Composition and Structure of the Management Committee
Committee Meetings and Issue of Minutes and Finance Reports
Maintenance of Accounting Records and Audit
HM Revenue & Customs
Review of Fees Charged to Persons or Organisations Hiring the Centre
Supervision of Organisations using the Centre on a Regular Basis
Cleaning and Caretakers
Liaison with Local Councillor
Security and Alarm Systems ;
Insurance including Employer’s Liability Insurance
Noise Consideration Issues Concerning Nearby Householders
Premises Bar Licence Summary
Repairs, Maintenance and Upkeep of the Centre
Service Contracts for Equipment Maintenance
Maintenance of an Asset and Equipment Register
Health and Safety Issues
The DECC Internet Website
The Future of the Centre including Review of Lease or Freehold Options
Any Other Items which the Holding Trustees Consider Relevant to the Good Management of the
Centre
Using this document as a guideline, I was able to discuss and obtain satisfactory answers to my questions
resulting in a comprehensive review of the administration and actual running of the Centre. I also carried
out a brief general inspection of the premises in conjunction with the officers of the Centre listed above.
Finally, from the answers and explanations received at the meeting, I am pleased to report that, in my
opinion, the Centre continues to be well run by an enthusiastic management team.
