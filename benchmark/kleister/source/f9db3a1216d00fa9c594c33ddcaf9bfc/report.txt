TRUSTEES' REPORT
FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 31 MAY 2017
The Trustees present their Annual Report and Accounts of the Charity for the period ended 31"May 2017
Principal Activity
The main objects of the charity are as follows;
A) To advance the Christian Faith by means of preaching the Gospel in the United Kingdom and other
parts of the world with a view to liberating minds, transforming lives and helping to fulfil dreams.
(B) To relieve persons who are in conditions of need or hardship and support Christian activities and
Christian organisations whose primary objectives are to engage in the conduct of Christian worship faith,
teaching and helping the needy,
(C) To advance education in accordance with Christian principles in the United Kingdom and other parts
of the world by establishing schools.
(D) To promote and fulfil such other charitable purposes beneficial to the communities as deemed necessary
by the trustees from time to time
Financial Review
The charity's balance sheet, as detailed on page 6, shows a net funds position of f3630.00
Review of Activities
The charity continues to run a weekly soup kitchen for the homeless in the local community
The Charity runs a women's group that supports women of all ages addressing issues like parenting,
loneliness, Career, personal and professional development. The Group runs a monthly breakfast meeting.
This year we also ran a Charity Variety show, where members of the public came together to enjoy a drink
and a variety of acts. Entry to the event was free to the Community.
The major source of our income is voluntary regular donations by members of the Charity
Total income for the year under review was f5,500.00 and total expenditure was f, 1,870.00
Risk Management
The trustees are aware of the risks which the charity faces and are continually taking steps to minimise
such risks.
Reserves
It is the policy of the Charity to maintain funds to meet with emergencies, administration cost and
support in other areas.
Investments Policy
There are no restrictions on the trustees' power to invest. There were no investments during the period
