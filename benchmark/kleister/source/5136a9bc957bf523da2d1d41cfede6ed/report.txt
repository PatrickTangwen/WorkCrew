TR STEES REPORT FOR YEAR ENQES 5th APRIL 2015
The Trustees, with the Church leaders and members celebrate another year in which
there have been many encouragements. There is a vibrancy in the fellowship with a
sense of expectation that new and exciting things are taking place. Visitors frequently
attend the Sunday meetings and several fainilies have joined us. The trustees are very
mindful of all the loving support given by dl and are pleased to acknowledge the
blessing of God on the work.
C.A .M.E.O (Come and meet each other) outreach group for the over fifhes continues,
meeting alternate weeks, with an attendance of between 20 and 30,
Again representatives froin the church were able to visit India, Thailand and Burma
where teaching and encouragement was given to local churches and groups of
Christians. Soine financial support was given to those churches overseas and to Hope
for Children in Latvia
Friday evening youth work continues and the trustees are very grateful to the
leadership and helpers.
'Love Beyond' (the musical) made it to Wembley Arena and those involved have
plans to promote the project on a wider basis but are in need of substantial financial
support.
In January 2015 a weekend away for the whole church was held at Pilgrim Hall,
Sussex where our principal 'values' ["not your average church"] were talked about
and discussed. Out of this came a picture of a heart surmounted by a crown. This gave
impetus later in the year when discussions took place about the future direction of the
church, a possible name change and re-shaping of the children's work —moving from
traditional Sunday school to 'Big Kids Church'
The Trustees appmve 'vision' of the Church and the direction given by the leadership
and are also satisfied with the finances. The Sunday morning meetings are attended
regularly by 80-100 people, including children; additionally, there are several groups
meeting throughout the week. The worship and musicians teain Me outstanding.
Thanks be to God for his continuing grace through another year.
